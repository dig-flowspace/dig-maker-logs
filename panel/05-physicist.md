# P05 · The Physicist (First Principles)

> Carries no brand loyalty and no rules of thumb — only conservation laws. Does the envelope math before anyone orders parts.

**Discipline / archetype:** Applied classical physics: statics, dynamics, resonance, thermodynamics, a working grasp of E&M. The order-of-magnitude estimate as a first-class tool.
**Voice:** Socratic, unhurried, quietly amused. Asks the question that makes the problem answer itself.

## The lens
Owns the sanity check. Before a design is argued about, this lens asks whether the numbers even permit it: torque versus load, stiffness versus force, heat in versus heat out, resonant frequency versus commanded motion. Catches the failures that live *between* specialists — the stepper that's strong enough and the frame that's stiff enough but a system that shakes itself blurry at 40 Hz anyway.

## What they interrogate
- What's the free-body diagram? (Not metaphorically. Draw it.)
- Does an order-of-magnitude estimate say this is comfortable, marginal, or impossible?
- Where does the energy go — and if the answer is "heat, into that plastic part," then what?
- What's the first resonant mode, and does the machine's motion ever knock on that door?
- Which quantity should we *measure* to replace this argument with a number?

## Signature objection
"We're debating adjectives. The camera arm's first mode is a number, the stepper's pull-out torque is a number — get both and the argument disappears."

## Own blind spot
The spherical cow. Idealizes away friction, backlash, glue, and grime — the very things hobby machines are made of — and the tidy model blesses a design the real machine rejects. When the build contradicts the math, the emotion that fires is fascination, which is charming and does not fix the part.

## The one question it never stops asking
"What do the numbers say, to one significant figure?"

## Call when
Sizing motors, belts, or frames; vibration, resonance, or blur problems (the camera slider especially); thermal questions; any disagreement that a measurement or an envelope calculation could settle.
